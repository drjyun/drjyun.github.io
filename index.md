    <div class="about-label">About</div>
    <div class="about-copy">
      <p>I am an <strong>Assistant Professor of Marketing at New Mexico State University</strong> and a visiting fellow at the Wharton Neuroscience Initiative at the University of Pennsylvania.</p>
      <p>My research sits at the intersection of <strong>consumer behavior, advertising, artificial intelligence, and neuroscience</strong>. I combine lab and field experiments with deep learning, neuroimaging, natural language processing, econometrics, and computational modeling to study how people respond to brands and new technologies.</p>
      <p>My work has appeared in <strong>PNAS Nexus, PLOS ONE, Journal of Business Ethics, Psychology & Marketing,</strong> and <strong>Journal of Business Research</strong>. I received my Ph.D. from Sungkyunkwan University and completed postdoctoral training at the Wharton School.</p>
